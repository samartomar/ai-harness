What should this folder be?

You ran npx @aihq/core --ui in a folder that is neither your organization’s policy repo nor a project. Pick what you came to do. aih remembers nothing about this choice.

Admin pageone or two people

Decide what the organization allows and publish <org>.policy.json. Best opened in a clone of your policy repo, so the name, id and repo fill themselves in from git.

Pick what your AI carries from what your organization allows, and save aih-project-policy.json into the project. The organization's policy is never changed. Best opened in the project folder.
