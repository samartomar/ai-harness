When something changes

The admin withdraws an item, or a new aih release lands: aih trims every policy built on it and tells each owner what went and why.

No fixed order: a developer needs no admin, an admin needs no review to publish, a team needs no setup. Nothing hard-coded: sources, categories, templates, for-whom labels and risk labels are rows in data files. A new aih release adds rows, not screens.
