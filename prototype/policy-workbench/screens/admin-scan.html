Do I need to run a scan?

What aih ships: no. Every item was scanned at its exact pinned version before the release, by aih built-in checks, Semgrep, SkillSpector and the Cisco scanner.

What Acme adds: yes. Each addition shows its own command. You run it, then load the result.

aih trust scan [owner/repo] --pin [commit] --apply

When a source updates

A scan belongs to one exact version. New version, new scan. You are asked again only about items whose content changed.
